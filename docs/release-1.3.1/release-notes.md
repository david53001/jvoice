## JVoice 1.3.1

**Less CPU, cooler Mac.** After you opened Settings once, the glowing "AI Polish" title kept animating in the hidden window — about a quarter of a CPU core, all the time, until JVoice was quit. It now pauses whenever Settings isn't on screen.

**Also fixed:**

- **AI Polish never changes a number.** If Claude's reply drops or rewrites a number from your dictation, JVoice pastes its own text instead.
- **Pasting into busy apps** (Chrome, Electron apps like the Claude desktop app) no longer sometimes pastes your old clipboard.

Your settings, custom words and permissions are kept.
